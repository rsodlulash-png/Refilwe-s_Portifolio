Featured mission

A seizure tracker for South Africa

Epilepsy can go unseen. Absence seizures are easy to miss, parents worry when children play outside, and doctors often work without recordings to decide on treatment. My 2050 Mission Impact Report describes a device that closes those gaps.

Detect
A small, waterproof patch worn on the head tracks brain activity, including absence seizures that are easily missed.
Alert
When a child has a seizure, an alert with their location goes to a parent's phone, so children can play outside more freely.
Protect
If a wearer has a seizure while driving, the device alerts emergency services.
Inform
Recordings are kept so doctors can identify the seizure type and stage, and choose the right medicine sooner.
